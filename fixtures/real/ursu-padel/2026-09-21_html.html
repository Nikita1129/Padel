Ursu Padel

Ursu Padel is a charming club located just outside the city, offering a peaceful and scenic setting perfect for enjoying padel and unwinding. With just one high-quality court, featuring a modern surface and professional equipment, the club provides an excellent playing experience. Surrounded by nature, the calm atmosphere makes it an ideal place to have fun, relax, and enjoy the game with friends. Whether you're an experienced player or new to padel, Ursu Padel guarantees a memorable and enjoyable experience!
